<Info>
  **Before you install: enable the MCP in your Klarity workspace.**

  1. Log in to Klarity in a browser.
  2. Go to **Settings → MCP**.
  3. Pick the workspace you want to expose to your assistant and complete the in-app setup.

  Sign-in will fail if you skip this. See [Authentication → Enable the MCP in your workspace](/authentication#enable-the-mcp-in-your-workspace) for the canonical reference, and [FAQ](/faq) for other common install issues.
</Info>

Once the Klarity connector is live in Anthropic's official directory:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open connector settings">
    In Claude, go to **Customize → Connectors**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Klarity">
    Search for **Klarity** and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    You will be redirected to Klarity to authenticate. Your assistant will then have access to your active workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your organization may need to approve the connector first, and you must be provisioned as a user in Klarity for the connection to succeed.
</Note>

## If you don't have access

If your organization hasn't approved the Klarity connector yet, or you can't see it in Claude's connector directory, install from one of the [other clients](/quickstart) instead. The data and permissions are identical regardless of how you connect.
