The Klarity MCP gives AI assistants direct access to your organization's business process intelligence. Connect it to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex, Gemini, or any [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/) client, and the assistant can query your processes, artifacts, observations, and knowledge graph in real time — grounded in how your business actually runs, not generic guesses.

The server is hosted at `https://architect-v2-api.klarity.ai/mcp`. You need a Klarity workspace to use it.

## Why connect it

Every question you'd ask the Klarity Advisor today works through the MCP. The unlock is surface area: the MCP embeds that intelligence inside an environment that can act on it — writing code, chaining with other connectors, building agents, and producing deliverables grounded in your real organizational data.

## What you get access to

Klarity organizes operational intelligence into three layers. The MCP gives you query access to all of them.

| Layer | What's in it | Example questions |
|---|---|---|
| **Process Index** | Process hierarchy, versions, steps, policies, dependencies, observations | "How does our vendor onboarding work?" |
| **Context Graph** | Entities, relationships, communities, claims extracted across the workspace | "What systems does the finance team depend on?" |
| **Artifacts** | Documents, videos, audio, diagrams, screenshots, logs — all searchable | "Find the latest SOX compliance checklist." |

See [Intelligence layers](/concepts/intelligence-layers) for the full mental model.

## What you can do

A human using the Advisor might run two or three tools per question. An LLM in an agentic loop can chain ten or more — searching, drilling in, cross-referencing, synthesizing. That's the unlock.
